# L-Nutra Voice of the Customer

A reporting site for L-Nutra's customer feedback (ProLon, Fast Bar, Fasting Shake, L-Protein, L-Pill, Guided Health, L-Nutra Health). It opens as an executive briefing, works as a triage inbox for the support team, and behind that is an analytics workstation: trends, themes, products, customers, regulatory clocks, forecasts and risk scores. Everything runs in the browser from plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; there is no build step, no account and no data leaves your machine. Mail comes in two ways: drop exported files onto the page, or run the small Python sidecar in `server/` that reads a mailbox over IMAP and feeds the site.

## Open it

- **Quickest**: double-click `index.html`. It runs from `file://` in Chrome, Safari or Firefox. Charts and fonts load from the internet; without a connection every chart shows its table twin instead.
- **Served** (needed for the mailbox connector): `python3 server/voc_server.py --no-poll`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:8765/>. `--no-poll` serves the site and API without touching any mailbox. Drop the flag once a mailbox is configured (see below).

The header dot tells you which mode you are in: grey **File mode** or **Static site** (no sidecar API), green **Sidecar** (connected), red when the sidecar stops answering. Click the dot to jump to Settings.

## The demo data

The site ships with a seeded, deterministic 12-month dataset generated by `tools/generate_seed.py` into `js/data.js`: about a year of synthetic customer mail, reviews, surveys and social posts from **Sep 15, 2025 to Sep 16, 2026**, plus customers, practitioner practices and monthly order counts. Names and addresses are invented.

The demo runs on a **demo clock**: every age, SLA, deadline and date preset counts from **Sep 16, 2026** (the "Demo clock · Sep 16, 2026" badge in the header). Switch to the real clock in Settings, or import mail newer than that date and the clock switches itself.

Planted incidents, so the detectors have something to find (Methods › Planted events lists all of them):

- Guided Health launch (Oct–Nov 2025) and Longevity Starter Pack launch (Aug 2026) as new-product emerging issues
- Black Friday / Cyber Monday surge, New Year reset wave and a September back-to-routine lift (volume anomalies)
- Holiday carrier incident (Dec 2025 shipping spike)
- Double-charge billing bug (Feb 2026, a P1 cluster within 72 hours)
- Foreign material in lot NG-0426-B (May 2026, food safety, P0) and an L-Drink leak ramp (Jul 2026)
- Next Gen taste drift (Jun–Jul 2026, sentiment decline)
- Six serious adverse events across the year, two inside the last ten business days (live MedWatch countdowns)
- A restricted queue of L-Nutra Health and practitioner-patient mail, and a handful of auto-replies, bounces and newsletters flagged as noise

Settings › **Reset to demo data** removes anything you imported and returns to this seed.

## Import real feedback

Drag files onto the drop zone in **Inbox** or **Settings › Import**, or click it to choose files. Accepted:

| Type | What happens |
|---|---|
| `.eml` | One email. Headers, encoded subjects, quoted-printable and base64 bodies, multipart (plain text preferred) |
| `.mbox` | A mailbox export (Apple Mail, Thunderbird, Google Takeout). Split into messages and processed like `.eml` |
| `.csv` | Help-desk exports (subject, body, from, dates, status), review exports (title, body, rating, product), monthly orders (month, product, orders) or the site's own record columns. Unknown headers open a column-mapping dialog and the mapping is remembered |
| `.json` | Records, `{records: [...]}`, raw emails, or a backup exported from Settings (records, overlays, settings, orders) |

Every message runs through the classifier (noise → language → sentiment → category → product → safety → urgency → entities; practitioner codes are `HCP-XXXXX`, practitioner order ids `PP-xxxxx`). Re-importing the same file adds nothing: duplicates are recognized by message id or a content fingerprint. The import summary reports added, duplicates, noise, needs-review and non-English counts.

Sample files to try live in `samples/`: `sample.eml`, `sample-quoted-rfc2047.eml`, `sample.mbox`, `sample-helpdesk.csv`, `sample-orders.csv`.

## Connect a mailbox

The sidecar (`server/voc_server.py`, Python 3.9 standard library, nothing to install) polls an IMAP folder read-only, cleans each message, drops auto-replies and newsletters, dedupes, and stores JSON under `~/Library/Application Support/voc/`. It never marks mail read, never deletes, and never stores a password. Full instructions, provider notes and troubleshooting: **[server/README.md](server/README.md)**.

Gmail or Google Workspace with an App Password, in five steps:

1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for the mailbox account, then create an App Password at <https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords> (Google shows it once; copy it without spaces). Make sure IMAP is enabled in Gmail settings.
2. Store the password outside the project: `security add-generic-password -s voc-mail -a support@l-nutra.com -w` (macOS Keychain), or export `VOC_MAIL_SECRET` in the shell that runs the server.
3. `cp server/config.example.json server/config.json` and set `username`, `folder` (INBOX or a Gmail label such as `VoC`) and `since` (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. `python3 server/voc_server.py`. It prints `TOKEN = …` and `URL = http://127.0.0.1:8765/`.
5. Open that URL, go to **Settings › Mailbox connector**, paste the token, click **Save token**, then **Sync now**. The header dot turns green and new mail appears in the Inbox on every poll.

Yahoo and iCloud work the same way with their app-specific passwords. Microsoft 365 does not (see Known limitations). Zapier, Make or Power Automate can post single messages to `POST /api/ingest` with the same token.

## What the analytics do

Every number carries its n, forecasts are shown as ranges, and the ƒ button on a tile or chart shows the formula. Methods explains each model.

- **Briefing**: headline, KPI strip with deltas versus the prior period, insight cards with "Show me" drill-downs, and what needs attention today.
- **Trends / Themes / Products / Customers**: volume, sentiment index, complaint rate, NPS/CSAT, share of voice, category and subcategory impact, product and kit-component breakdowns, lot tracking, customer segments and practitioner mail.
- **Forecast**: Holt-Winters projection of contact volume with an honest interval, back-tested on the seed.
- **Anomalies**: day-of-week adjusted spikes and dips in volume or category share, flagged as warning or critical.
- **Emerging issues**: subcategories, lots, components and phrases that ramp over the last weeks compared with their own history.
- **Churn risk**: per-customer score from recency, complaint history, sentiment and subscription state, with tiers, reasons and revenue at risk.
- **Escalation risk**: open records ranked by the chance they escalate, from urgency, sentiment, age against SLA and category.
- **Predicted CSAT**: ridge regression on resolved records, applied to open ones, so the team knows which open cases are heading for a bad survey.
- **Drivers and what-changed**: which categories, products and segments moved the sentiment index and complaint rate between periods.
- **Regulatory clocks**: MedWatch 15-business-day countdowns for serious adverse events (the statutory filing applies to the L-Pill as a dietary supplement; on ProLon kits and bars the same clock tracks a voluntary report) and 24-hour Reportable Food Registry clocks for food-safety reports on conventional food and dietary supplements, with US federal holidays accounted for. The deadline table labels a voluntary MedWatch row as such.
- **Alerts**: threshold rules (P0 open, SLA breaches, spikes, serious AE received, emerging lots) with acknowledge and snooze.
- **Reports**: weekly and monthly report previews you can print, save through the sidecar, and carry actions forward from.

## Run the tests

JavaScript modules run headless under macOS JavaScriptCore (12 test files under `tests/`); the Python sidecar has a unittest suite (32 cases).

```bash
bash tests/run.sh                             # every tests/test_*.js (12 files), exits non-zero on any failure
python3 -m unittest server/test_voc_server.py  # sidecar: quote stripping, normalizing, noise, token, pagination (32 tests)
```

`tests/run.sh` loads the pure modules (including `js/charts.js`) before each test file and skips a missing module with a warning. Regenerate the seed with `python3 tools/generate_seed.py` (deterministic and byte-identical across runs; `tests/test_seed_shape.js` checks its shape and `tests/test_seed_agreement.js` checks classifier agreement, the sentiment mix and NPS, product realism, the fitted churn model and that the planted events are detected). `VOC_SEED_DIAG=diag.json python3 tools/generate_seed.py` also writes which templates built each record, for per-template agreement checks.


## Privacy and PHI

Everything the browser imports stays in the browser (IndexedDB and localStorage on this machine); the sidecar stores its JSON under `~/Library/Application Support/voc/`, outside the project folder, so it is not synced by iCloud Drive or zipped up with the site. Mail addressed to restricted recipients (`Med.Ed@l-nutra.com` by default) or from L-Nutra Health / practitioner patients is marked **restricted**: the site shows it redacted unless you open the restricted queue, and the sidecar can redact it before it is stored. Client-side redaction is a display convenience, not access control. HIPAA likely applies to L-Nutra Health patient mail and GDPR to EU/UK senders; keep that mail in a separate mailbox or enable server-side redaction, and keep the browser profile and data directory on a managed machine.

## Known limitations

- **Microsoft 365 / Outlook.com** cannot connect yet: Microsoft disabled password IMAP logins, so the connector needs OAuth (device-code flow with XOAUTH2, or Microsoft Graph). The `microsoft365` preset only fills in the host. Until then, forward mail to a Gmail label, export `.eml`/`.mbox`, or post messages to `/api/ingest`.
- **Heuristic scores are not probabilities.** Churn risk, escalation risk, predicted CSAT and the classifier's confidence are rule-based or fitted on the seed; treat them as rankings and starting points, not as calibrated odds.
- Sentiment and categories come from a lexicon and regex rules, not a language model. Non-English mail is left unscored. Manual corrections in the Inbox override the rules and are kept in an audit trail.
- The forecast assumes weekly seasonality and no knowledge of promotions; it is a baseline to judge surprises against.
- Charts need Chart.js from a CDN; offline, every chart falls back to its table.
- Browser storage is per browser profile. Export a backup from Settings before clearing site data or moving to another machine.
